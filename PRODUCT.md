# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Bali who want nearby coffee without waiting in an ordering queue: local commuters, students, workers, social-discovery visitors, and visitors unfamiliar with the area.

## Product Purpose

Boxling helps people understand the mobile coffee-cart concept, find an operating cart, see cart-relevant availability, order through the application, and collect the order themselves.

## Positioning

Modern roaming coffee carts in Bali, paired with an app that makes the nearest useful pickup point easier to find and order from. Boxling is pickup-only, not delivery.

## Operating Context

Customers commonly discover Boxling while moving through Bali, through street visibility, social media, search, or QR codes. Mobile speed, clear directions, current operating information, and a low-friction explanation of pickup matter more than long brand storytelling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Default language is Bahasa Indonesia; English localization is planned.
- The website is an acquisition and discovery layer; the application is the transactional layer.
- Location access must be requested only after a clear user action and manual area search must remain available.
- Store URLs, active cart locations, operating hours, prices, ratings, and operational metrics are not yet verified and must not be fabricated.
- Every ordering message must explicitly distinguish self-pickup from delivery.

## Brand Commitments

- Production name: Boxling Coffee & Milkbar.
- Primary market: Bali, Indonesia.
- Existing red and white Boxling marks in `public/brand/` are authoritative assets.
- Product language should be direct, useful, energetic, and locally legible.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product requirements: `docs/PRD-Boxling-Landing-App.md`.
- Existing Boxling wordmarks and icons: `public/brand/`.
- Existing Boxling photography/video: `public/media/`.
- No verified public testimonials, active location list, prices, or app-store destinations are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Location first.
- Pickup clarity in every conversion path.
- Real operational data over decorative claims.
- Fast mobile discovery.
- Explain app value before applying download pressure.
